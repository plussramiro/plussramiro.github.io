During my research stay with Jimena Berni and her team at the University of Sussex, I explored and compared larval and adult Drosophila connectomes to investigate the neural principles underlying locomotion. This work brought together complex network analysis, computational neuroscience, and 3D visualization of neuronal circuits.

I reflect on the experience, the ideas we shared, and the opportunity to continue collaborating.
