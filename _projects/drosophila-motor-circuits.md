I began this project during my September 2026 research visit to [Dr. Jimena Berni's lab](https://www.bsms.ac.uk/about/contact-us/staff/dr-jimena-berni.aspx) at Brighton and Sussex Medical School, University of Sussex. I continue to collaborate remotely with the lab on larval and adult _Drosophila_ motor circuits, exploring how their organization relates to locomotion across developmental stages.

Working with Jimena, I am comparing larval and adult motor circuits to explore how their organization supports rhythmic activity and locomotion, and which principles of motor control may be shared across developmental stages. I used napari to inspect larval neuronal morphologies in their anatomical context and explored connectivity patterns as part of this ongoing work. The two views above show the same selected circuits from different orientations, helping relate their projections to segmental anatomy. Our continuing collaboration examines how shared and distinct features of circuit organization relate to the different motor demands of larvae and adults.
